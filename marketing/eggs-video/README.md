# "We don't want eggs" video

20-second vertical video (1080×1920, MP4) for WhatsApp Status, TikTok, Reels and Discord: the CS2 egg update ("drop, hatch, feed") vs. what players want, Premier tier colours, and a call to action to **cleanlobby.com**. English and French from the same file.

- Text: the `T` object in `video.html` (`en` and `fr`). Scene timings: `SCENES`, animations: `render(t)`.
- Preview stills: `node render.mjs still --lang fr --preview 2,9,19.5`
- Render: `node render.mjs out.mp4 --lang fr` (or `--lang en`). Needs Google Chrome and `ffmpeg-static` (`npm install --no-save --no-package-lock ffmpeg-static`).
- Rendered copies: `C:\Users\PC\Videos\CleanLobby-oeufs-FR.mp4` and `CleanLobby-eggs-EN.mp4`.

Not part of the website; nothing here is deployed.

How CleanLobby works

  1. Sign in through Steam. Every account is a real, proven Steam account. CleanLobby never sees your Steam password and has no access to your inventory or trades.
  2. Pass the trust checks. Steam account at least 2 years old, at least 500 hours of CS2, no recent VAC or game ban.
  3. Build your five. Create a team and invite players, or ask to join a team that is recruiting.
  4. Queue as a full team. CleanLobby matches you with another complete 5-stack from your region with a similar CS2 Premier rating.
  5. Play through CS2 Private Matchmaking. One captain shares the private matchmaking code, both teams join on Valve servers, then the captains report the score.
  6. Build your reputation. Players rate each other after the match. The CleanLobby Trust Score combines Steam history, ratings, reliability and match record.

Regions: North Africa (Morocco, Algeria, Tunisia, Libya, Egypt), Europe, Middle East, Africa, the Americas, Asia and Oceania. CleanLobby is a reputation layer, not an anti-cheat.

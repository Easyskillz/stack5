🔐 Is this safe?

You sign in on Steam's own website. Before typing your password or scanning the QR code, check that the address bar says https://steamcommunity.com.
  • STACK5 only receives your public SteamID. We never see your password.
  • STACK5 can't access your inventory, your skins or your trades.
  • STACK5 will never ask for your Steam Guard code, an API key or your trade link.
  • Approving in the Steam app? Steam shows where the request comes from. If it isn't where you are, deny it.

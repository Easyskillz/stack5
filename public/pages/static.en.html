How CleanLobby works

  1. Sign in through Steam. Every account is a real, proven Steam account. CleanLobby never sees your Steam password and has no access to your inventory or trades.
  2. Pass the trust checks. Steam account at least 2 years old, at least 500 hours of CS2, no recent VAC or game ban.
  3. Build your five. Create a team and invite players, or ask to join a team that is recruiting.
  4. Queue as a full team. CleanLobby matches you with another complete 5-stack close enough for good ping, with a similar CS2 Premier rating. Teammates find each other by the languages they speak.
  5. Play through CS2 Private Matchmaking. One captain shares the private matchmaking code, both teams join on Valve servers, then the captains report the score.
  6. Build your reputation. After the match, players vote 👍/👎 on each other’s comms, teamplay, attitude and sportsmanship (skill is the CS2 Premier rating). The CleanLobby Trust Score (0–100) combines Steam account history (35%), votes from teammates and opponents (30%), reliability (25%) and matches played (10%). 60 and above is good, 75 and above is excellent. Raise it by playing matches to the end, rating everyone after each match and accepting matches in time.

Beta countries: Morocco, France, Belgium, Switzerland, Luxembourg, Monaco, Spain, Andorra, Portugal, the United Kingdom, Ireland and Malta. More countries later. Languages: English, French, Spanish and Portuguese. CS2 Premier ratings are read from Leetify. Coming soon: Premium officiated matches on private servers with a real admin who can ask any player to stream their screen and can kick cheaters, plus 5v5 tournaments. CleanLobby is a reputation layer, not an anti-cheat.

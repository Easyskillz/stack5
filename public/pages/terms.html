3. Requirements to play

To keep new and throwaway accounts out of matches, creating, joining or queueing a team requires that your linked Steam account:

  • is at least 2 years old;
  • has at least 500 hours of CS2 playtime;

We check these automatically using public Steam data and may re-check them at any time. We may change these requirements, add others (for example FACEIT or Premier history), or approve or refuse individual players at our discretion.

  • Declining found matches, letting them expire, or leaving a team while it's queued lowers your reliability score.
  • CleanLobby finds and confirms opponents. Teams arrange and play the match themselves. We don't run game servers and aren't responsible for what happens in a match.
